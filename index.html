How it works

Five stages, all online

  1. Stage 1

    Send brief

    Share plot size, location and what you want to build, by WhatsApp or email.

  2. Stage 2

    Site details

    Send photos, a sketch or any existing plan. We confirm the measurements with you.

  3. Stage 3

    Drawings

    You receive plans and elevations as files and we revise them until you approve.

  4. Stage 4

    Estimate

    A costed list of quantities so you can budget before work begins.

  5. Stage 5

    Supervision

    Guidance and quality checks while the building goes up.

The practice

About

Soren Construction World is a construction consultancy in Bhubaneswar run by Er. Bhola Sankar Soren. The practice is more than a year old and works by phone, WhatsApp and email, so clients do not need to visit an office to get a building planned.

The aim is simple: every question about a building, from the first sketch to the last coat of paint, has one place to be asked.
